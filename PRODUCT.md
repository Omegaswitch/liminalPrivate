# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Keyboard collectors deciding whether to buy GMK Liminal, a keycap set sold through a group buy. They judge colorways and renders closely before committing. (Confirmed with the owner.)

## Product Purpose

The promotional site for GMK Liminal, a keycap set themed on the Backrooms. A visit opens straight on the kits page: colorway, kits, renders, vendors (this copy has no film). Success for a visit is that the visitor trusts the set's look. (Confirmed.)

## Positioning

The set's colors and imagery are taken from the building itself (Level 0: carpet, wallpaper, fluorescent light). The renders show keyboards half sunk into that building. The Backrooms is treated as architecture, not horror. (From the site copy and docs/concept.md; the architecture framing is not re-confirmed.)

## Operating Context

- Static site, no backend. The kits page is the whole site.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Collects nothing: no accounts, forms, analytics, cookies, local storage or third-party requests. The privacy policy (`privacy.html`) says so; keep it true or update the policy first. CSP allows the site's own origin only.
- Honors `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Set facts that are confirmed: the keycap design. Not yet final: the four colors (Alphas #e5d382, Mods #b1a057, Accents #ffd03b, Legends #393b3b, code N9; Pantone matches are approximations). Still TBC: kit contents beyond the seven names (Base, Noúmeno alpha, Novelties, Normies, Weird (formerly Spacebars), Deskmat 1, Deskmat 2), all prices, vendor links, profile, material, designer, group-buy dates. Never present a TBC fact as final.
- Open: whether "design is confirmed" in the owner's answer covers the renders as well as the keycap design.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: GMK Liminal. Wordmark: `public/logo.png`.
- From docs/concept.md, not re-confirmed: copy written like an asset register; never "welcome to the Backrooms" or creepypasta language.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real: the hero render and five renders in `public/renders/`, kitting sheets for the five kits in `public/kits/` (deskmats have none yet), the four swatches (provisional), the vendor name list (six vendors, no links).
- Absent, so do not fabricate: prices, vendor URLs, testimonials, specs, group-buy dates, designer credit.

## Product Principles

1. Accuracy of the set's look comes before spectacle: colors and renders must be trustworthy.
2. The buying information stays reachable at all times.
3. Placeholders are marked as placeholders; nothing unconfirmed reads as final.
4. The site collects nothing about its visitors.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Reduced-motion and keyboard access are in place (Skip, Esc, focus rings). The WCAG AA target in docs/concept.md has not been re-confirmed.
