Privacy policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

This policy covers the GMK Liminal website. In short: the site does not collect, store or share personal information about you and runs no analytics or tracking. It sets no cookies.

Who runs this site

The site is owned and run by Omegaswitch. For any question about this policy, contact Omegaswitch.

What the site collects

Nothing. The site has no accounts, no forms, no comments, no newsletter sign-up and no checkout. It does not use local storage, analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, social media embeds or fingerprinting.

What happens in your browser

The page is plain text and pictures. Nothing about how long you stay, how far you scroll or what you click is sent anywhere, and the site never uses your camera or microphone.

The site makes no requests to other companies' servers. Fonts and images are served from this site, not from a font or image service.

Server logs

Like any website, the site's host, Vercel, may automatically record standard technical details of each request: your IP address, the page requested, the time, and your browser's user agent. These logs are kept by Vercel for security and to keep the service running, under Vercel's privacy policy. They are not used to identify you, build a profile or show you advertising.

Links to other sites

The vendor links take you to shops run by other companies. Once you follow one, that site's own privacy policy applies, including anything you buy there.

Children

The site is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If the site starts collecting anything, for example to run the group buy, this page will be updated before that happens, and the date at the top will change.
